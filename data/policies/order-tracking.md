# Tracking an Order

Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed to the customer and also appears under "My Orders" in their account. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to become active in the carrier's system after a shipping label is created, so a tracking number that shows no movement on the first day is normal and not a sign of a problem.

Orders typically move through four statuses: Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered. An order stuck in "Processing" for more than 3 business days should be flagged for manual review, as this usually indicates a warehouse stock issue.

Guest orders can be tracked using the order number and the email address used at checkout, via the "Track an Order" page, without needing to log in.
