# Requesting an Invoice or Receipt

A basic order receipt is automatically emailed at the time of purchase and is also available under My Orders > Order Details > Download Receipt. This covers the needs of most personal customers.

Customers who need a formal invoice with their business name, tax ID, or a specific billing format for expense reporting can request one through support, providing the order number and the billing details to include. Formal invoices are typically issued within 2 business days.

Invoices cannot be backdated or altered to show a different purchase date, amount, or billing name than what was recorded at the time of the original transaction.
