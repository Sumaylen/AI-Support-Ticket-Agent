# General Refund Policy

Most items purchased from Meridian Goods can be refunded within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the order number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout; Meridian Goods does not offer cash refunds or refunds to a different card.

Sale items and clearance items are eligible for store credit only, not a refund to the original payment method, unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

Digital products, gift cards, and personalized/custom items are final sale and not eligible for refund except in cases of a confirmed defect or an error made by Meridian Goods.
