# Shipping Options and Costs

Meridian Goods offers three domestic shipping speeds: Standard (5-7 business days, free on orders over $50), Expedited (2-3 business days, flat $9.99), and Overnight (next business day if ordered before 1pm local time, flat $24.99). Shipping speed is selected at checkout and cannot be changed once the order has been placed.

Overnight shipping is not available for orders shipping to PO boxes or military APO/FPO addresses; these addresses are automatically limited to Standard or Expedited shipping at checkout.

Orders containing items from multiple warehouses may arrive in separate packages on different days, even if a single shipping speed was selected. This is normal and does not indicate an error with the order.
