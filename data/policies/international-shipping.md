# International Shipping

Meridian Goods currently ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the European Union. International orders take 10-20 business days to arrive, depending on customs processing in the destination country, and are not eligible for Expedited or Overnight shipping.

Import duties, customs fees, and local taxes are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the order total at checkout. These fees are set by the destination country's customs authority and Meridian Goods has no ability to estimate, waive, or refund them.

International orders cannot be shipped to freight forwarders or package consolidation services; orders identified as going to one of these addresses may be cancelled and refunded as a precaution against fraud.
