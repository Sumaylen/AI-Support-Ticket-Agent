# Payment Security and Card Storage

Meridian Goods does not store full credit card numbers on its own servers. Saved cards are handled entirely through a certified third-party payment processor, and only the card's last four digits and expiration month/year are visible to support staff or shown in the customer's account.

Customers can remove a saved card at any time from Account Settings > Payment Methods. Removing a saved card does not affect any orders that were already placed using that card.

Support staff can never view, request, or accept a customer's full card number, CVV code, or online banking password over chat, email, or phone. Any message claiming to be from Meridian Goods that asks for this information directly is not legitimate.
