# Resetting a Password

Customers who forget their password can request a reset link from the login page by entering their account email. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes and can only be used once. If the link expires, a new one must be requested.

For security, Meridian Goods never sends a password directly by email, text, or phone, and support staff can never see or tell a customer their current password. If a customer suspects unauthorized access to their account, they should reset their password immediately and contact support so the account can be reviewed for suspicious order activity.

Accounts that have three failed login attempts within 15 minutes are temporarily locked for 30 minutes as a security measure. This lock cannot be lifted early, even by support staff.
