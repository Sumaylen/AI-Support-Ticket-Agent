# Creating an Account

Anyone can create a Meridian Goods account for free from the sign-up page using an email address and password, or by linking a Google or Apple account. A verification email is sent immediately after sign-up and must be confirmed within 24 hours, or the account will need to be re-registered.

Guest checkout is available for customers who prefer not to create an account, but guest orders cannot be tracked through the "My Orders" dashboard and do not accumulate loyalty points. Customers who checked out as a guest can convert their order history into a full account within 30 days of purchase by creating an account with the same email address used at checkout.

Each customer may hold only one active account per email address. Duplicate accounts found using the same payment method or shipping address may be merged or flagged for review.
