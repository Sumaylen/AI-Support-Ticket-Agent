# How to Contact Support

Support is available by live chat and email, Monday through Friday, 8am-8pm, and Saturday, 9am-5pm (customer's local time zone). Live chat typically has a response time under 5 minutes during business hours; email requests are answered within 1 business day.

Order-related requests are resolved fastest when the customer provides their order number up front, since account lookup by name or email alone can be slower and less reliable, particularly for guest orders.

Meridian Goods does not offer phone support at this time. Any communication claiming to be a Meridian Goods support phone line should be treated as suspicious and not engaged with.
