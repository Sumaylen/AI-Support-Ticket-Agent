# What Qualifies for a Refund

Items are eligible for a refund if they are unused, in resellable condition, and returned within 30 days of delivery in their original packaging with all included accessories. Worn clothing, used electronics, and opened hygiene or personal care products are not eligible for refund due to health and resale restrictions, except in cases of a manufacturing defect.

Items damaged during return shipping due to inadequate packaging by the customer may have the refund amount reduced to reflect the damage, at the discretion of the warehouse inspection team.

A missing original receipt or packing slip does not disqualify a refund as long as the order number can be found in the customer's account or verified by email.
