# How Long Refunds Take

Once a returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse, refunds are processed within 3 business days. After the refund is issued, it typically takes an additional 5-10 business days for the funds to appear on the customer's statement, depending on their bank or card issuer.

Refunds for orders paid by store credit or gift card are typically faster, appearing back on the account within 24 hours of processing, since no third-party bank is involved.

If more than 10 business days have passed since a refund was confirmed as processed and the customer still has not received it, support should ask for the last four digits of the payment method and the refund confirmation date to investigate with the payment processor.
