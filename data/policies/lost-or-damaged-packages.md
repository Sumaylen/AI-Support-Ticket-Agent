# Lost or Damaged Packages

A package is considered "lost" if tracking shows no movement for more than 7 business days for domestic orders, or 15 business days for international orders. Lost package claims should be filed through support, which will open an investigation with the carrier; this typically takes 5-7 business days to resolve.

Packages that arrive visibly damaged should be photographed before opening, if possible, and reported to support within 48 hours of delivery. A replacement or refund can usually be issued without waiting for a carrier investigation if photo evidence is provided.

Meridian Goods is not responsible for packages confirmed as delivered by tracking to the correct address but reported missing by the customer (for example, taken from a porch); these cases should be reported to local authorities, and support can provide a copy of the delivery confirmation to assist.
