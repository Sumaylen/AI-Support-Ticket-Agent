# Closing or Deleting an Account

Customers can request account deletion from the Account Settings page or by contacting support. Deletion requests are processed within 14 days. Order history required for tax, warranty, or legal record-keeping purposes is retained for 7 years even after an account is deleted, in line with financial recordkeeping requirements, but is no longer visible to the customer.

An account cannot be deleted while it has an order in progress, an open return, or an unresolved payment dispute. The customer will be asked to wait until these are resolved before the deletion can proceed.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Loyalty points, saved payment methods, and saved addresses are all erased and cannot be recovered, including by support staff.
