# Accepted Payment Methods

Meridian Goods accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards, as well as PayPal and Apple Pay. Bank transfers and cryptocurrency are not currently supported as payment methods.

Gift cards issued by Meridian Goods can be combined with one other payment method on the same order but cannot be used to purchase additional gift cards. Store credit issued from a refund can be used the same way.

A single order can use only one card or one PayPal account as the primary payment method; splitting a single order's payment across two different credit cards is not supported at checkout.
