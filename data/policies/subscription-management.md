# Managing a Subscription Box

Meridian Goods' monthly subscription box charges on the same calendar day each month, based on the date of the first order. Customers can pause a subscription for up to 3 consecutive months from Account Settings > Subscriptions without losing their subscriber pricing or box customization preferences.

Changing the contents or size of an upcoming box must be done at least 3 days before the next billing date. Changes made after that cutoff will apply starting with the following month's box instead.

A skipped or paused month does not extend the subscriber discount eligibility period; discounts are based on consecutive months of active billing, not total months since sign-up.
