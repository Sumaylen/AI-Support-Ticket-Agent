# Feedback and Formal Complaints

General product or service feedback can be submitted through the Feedback form on the website, or shared directly with a support agent during a chat or email conversation. Feedback submissions do not receive an individual reply unless the customer specifically requests a follow-up.

Formal complaints, including those about a support interaction itself, are escalated to a team lead rather than handled by the original support agent, and are acknowledged within 2 business days.

Complaints involving a safety issue with a product (for example, an electrical or choking hazard) are treated as high priority and escalated immediately, regardless of normal response-time targets, and may result in the product being flagged for review independent of the individual customer's case.
