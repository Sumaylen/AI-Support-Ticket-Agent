# Cancelling an Order

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund only while they are still in "Processing" status. Once an order moves to "Shipped," it can no longer be cancelled and must instead be returned after delivery following the standard return process.

To cancel an eligible order, the customer can use the "Cancel Order" button under My Orders, or contact support directly. Cancellations initiated by support are typically completed within a few minutes, but refunds still follow the standard refund timeline once processed.

Orders containing personalized or made-to-order items cannot be cancelled once production has started, even if the order still shows as "Processing." These items are marked as "Custom" on the product page at the time of purchase.
