# When a Payment Fails

A payment can fail for several common reasons: insufficient funds, an expired card, an incorrect billing address, or the card issuer declining the transaction for its own fraud-prevention reasons. Meridian Goods does not receive a specific reason from the card network in most cases, only that the payment was declined.

When a payment fails, the order is held in "Payment Pending" status for 24 hours, during which the customer can update their payment method to complete the order. If no valid payment is provided within 24 hours, the order is automatically cancelled and any reserved inventory is released.

Customers whose payments repeatedly fail should be advised to contact their bank directly, as Meridian Goods support cannot see the specific decline reason or override a bank's decision.
