# Cancelling a Subscription

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Account Settings > Subscriptions, with no cancellation fee. Cancelling stops all future billing but does not refund the most recent charge if a box has already been prepared for shipment.

To avoid being charged for a box the customer does not want, cancellation must be completed at least 3 days before the next billing date; the same cutoff used for making changes to an upcoming box.

Cancelled subscribers who resubscribe within 60 days will automatically regain their previous subscriber-tier discount. After 60 days, the account is treated as a new subscription and starts at standard introductory pricing.
