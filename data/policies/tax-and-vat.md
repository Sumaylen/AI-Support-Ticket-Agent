# Sales Tax and VAT

Sales tax is calculated automatically at checkout based on the shipping address and applicable local tax rates, and is shown as a separate line item before the order is placed. Meridian Goods cannot manually adjust or waive sales tax for an order, even for tax-exempt organizations; tax-exempt customers must apply for exempt status in advance through the Tax Exemption page before placing an order.

For orders shipping to the European Union and United Kingdom, VAT is included in the displayed product price rather than added at checkout, in line with local pricing regulations.

Tax amounts already charged on a completed order can only be adjusted as part of a refund; support cannot issue a standalone tax-only refund on an order that was not otherwise returned or cancelled.
