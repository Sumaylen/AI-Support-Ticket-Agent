=====================Metric definitions=====================
- Hit@k: 1 if at least one expected file appears in the top k, otherwise 0. This is the most forgiving metric: did we get anything useful?

- Recall@k: fraction of expected files that appear in the top k. This asks: did we find everything we needed?

- Precision@k: fraction of the top k chunks that came from an expected file. This asks: how much of what we retrieved is useful, and how much is noise?
