1.What payment methods does Meridian Goods accept?

Expected answer:
Meridian Goods accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards, as well as PayPal and Apple Pay. Bank transfers and cryptocurrency are not supported. Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method, but a single order can use only one card or one PayPal account as the primary payment method.

2.Can I get a refund if I return an item after 30 days?

Expected answer:
Most items can be refunded within 30 days of delivery if they are unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number. Sale items and clearance items are only eligible for store credit unless the item arrived damaged or defective. Digital products, gift cards, and personalized/custom items are final sale unless there is a confirmed defect or merchant error.

3.Can I change my shipping address after I place an order?

Expected answer:
Shipping address changes can only be made while an order is in Processing status. Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed. In that case, the customer should contact the shipping carrier directly or arrange to intercept the package if the carrier allows it.

4.Can I add or remove items from an order after it has been placed?

Expected answer:
No. Adding or removing items from an existing order is not supported. A customer who wants to add an item must place a new, separate order. A customer who wants to remove an item must wait for delivery and process a partial return.

5.What shipping options are available, and when is overnight shipping unavailable?

Expected answer:
Meridian Goods offers Standard shipping (5–7 business days, free on orders over $50), Expedited shipping (2–3 business days, $9.99), and Overnight shipping (next business day if ordered before 1pm local time, $24.99). Overnight shipping is not available for orders shipping to PO boxes or military APO/FPO addresses; those addresses are limited to Standard or Expedited shipping.
